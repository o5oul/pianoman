# pianoman [![View PCB on KiCanvas](https://hack.club/pcb-badge)](https://kicanvas.org/?repo=https://github.com/<OWNER>/<REPOSITORY>/tree/main/pcb)

## **Notes:**
- PCB can be purchased from JLPCB or PCBway, all parts will be listed + sample purchase links will be given.
- Switches should be PCB mounted, as there is no plate to hold the switches in place.

## **Build instructions:**
- Begin by inserting five (5) M2 screws into the base, then connecting them to the M2 standoffs through the other end.
- Solder the Seeed Studio XIAO ESP32 onto the given space.
- Solder the switches and rotary encoder onto the PCB.
- Once the soldering is completed, connect the PCB to a PC and write on the firmware.
